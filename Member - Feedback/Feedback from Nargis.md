## Individual Reflection

Working with my group taught me the importance of communication and shared responsibility. We helped each other solve problems and connect different parts of the project. Sometimes, combining our work was challenging, but discussing the issues helped us move forward.

My main contribution was developing the frontend and user interface. I worked on the homepage, account forms, clothing listings, search filters, and dashboard interfaces.

One challenge was connecting the pages to the backend and making sure buttons and status updates worked correctly. Testing helped me identify and fix these problems.

This project improved my HTML, CSS, and JavaScript skills and taught me how to collaborate with a backend developer. In the future, I would improve the design further through user feedback and more testing on different screen sizes.
