# Member Feedback

Being part of this project was a really great experience for me. I enjoyed working with my team, sharing ideas, helping each other, and seeing how all our individual work came together to build the final project.

As the backend developer, I mainly worked on the Flask routes, database, and the functionality behind the website. I learned a lot about connecting the frontend with the backend, working with SQLite, handling user accounts and sessions, managing listings, and making different features work together. I also learned how important testing and communication are, especially when different parts of the project depend on each other.

I also got a better understanding of how Scrum works in a team project, including working in sprints, dividing tasks, and improving the project step by step.

Overall, I truly enjoyed working with my team. We supported each other and worked together to complete the project. I am happy with what we achieved, and this project gave me valuable practical experience and helped me become more confident in backend development.
