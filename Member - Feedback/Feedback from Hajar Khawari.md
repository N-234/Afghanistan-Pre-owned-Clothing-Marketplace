# My Experience as a Scrum Master  

Working on this project was a valuable experience for me. As the Scrum Master, my main responsibility was to organize our Scrum meetings, follow the progress of each Sprint, track blockers, prepare meeting documentation, and help the team stay organized throughout the project.

One of the most important things I learned was how much communication matters in a team project. We had different responsibilities, but many tasks depended on each other. Because of this, we had to stay in contact, share updates, and help each other whenever a problem came up.

During the project, I also worked on Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum meetings, Sprint Reviews, Retrospectives, Sprint Backlogs, progress tracking, Burndown Charts, and other Scrum documentation. This helped me understand how Scrum works in a real project, not only in theory.

Of course, we also faced some challenges. Sometimes development took more time than expected, some features needed extra testing, and we had to adjust our plans during a Sprint. These situations taught me how important it is to stay flexible, communicate clearly, and keep checking the team's progress.

What I appreciated most was our teamwork. Sara, Nargis, and Parwana were supportive, responsible, and willing to help when something was difficult. We worked together, shared ideas, solved problems, and continued until the project was completed.

Overall, I am really happy with what we achieved as a team. This project improved my understanding of Scrum, teamwork, communication, planning, and project coordination. It also gave me more confidence in working with a team and managing a software project step by step.
