# Project Overview

---

**Project Title:** Afghan Girls' Pre-Owned Clothes Marketplace

---

## 1.1 Description

The Afghan Girls' Pre-Owned Clothes Marketplace is a web-based platform designed to provide a simple, organized, and accessible way for girls to buy and sell pre-owned clothes online. The system will connect buyers and sellers through one platform where users can browse available clothes, search for specific items, view product details, and contact sellers.

The idea behind this system is to create a dedicated marketplace for pre-owned clothes instead of relying on general social media platforms or informal communication groups. In informal marketplaces, users may have difficulty finding specific clothes, comparing prices, checking product information, or managing their own listings. This system will organize the buying and selling process by providing structured information for each clothing item.

Sellers will be able to create an account and upload clothing items they want to sell. They can provide important information such as the clothing name, category, size, condition, price, description, location, and images. Sellers will also be able to edit or remove their listings and mark items as sold when they are no longer available.

Buyers will be able to browse available clothes and use search and filtering options to find products that match their needs. They will be able to filter clothes according to factors such as category, size, price, and condition. Buyers can also view detailed information about a product before deciding whether to contact the seller.

The system will include an administrator role to manage the platform. Administrators will be able to manage user accounts, monitor clothing listings, review reported content, and remove inappropriate or invalid listings. This will help maintain an organized and safe marketplace.

The project will be developed using Software Engineering principles and the Scrum framework. The system will be developed incrementally through multiple sprints. Each sprint will include planning, development, testing, daily Scrum meetings, Sprint Review, and Sprint Retrospective. The team's progress and project documentation will be maintained throughout the development process.

---

## 1.2 Problem Statement

Buying and selling pre-owned clothes can be difficult when there is no dedicated and organized platform for users. People may depend on social media pages, messaging groups, or personal contacts to find or sell second-hand clothes.

These methods can create several problems. Clothing posts can quickly become difficult to find because they are mixed with other content. Sellers may not provide complete information about the clothes, and buyers may have difficulty searching for items based on their preferred size, price, category, or condition.

There is also no organized way for sellers to manage their clothing listings or for administrators to monitor inappropriate or misleading content.

Therefore, this project proposes a dedicated web-based marketplace where girls can buy, sell, search, and manage pre-owned clothes in an organized environment.

---

## 1.3 Objectives

The main objective of the project is to develop a user-friendly web-based marketplace that makes buying and selling pre-owned clothes easier and more organized.

The specific objectives are:

1. To provide an online platform where girls can buy and sell pre-owned clothes.
2. To allow users to create and manage their own accounts.
3. To allow sellers to create, edit, and remove clothing listings.
4. To provide buyers with clear information about available clothes.
5. To provide search and filtering functions to help buyers find suitable products.
6. To allow buyers to contact sellers regarding available products.
7. To allow sellers to mark clothing items as sold when they are no longer available.
8. To provide administrators with tools to manage users and clothing listings.
9. To provide a simple and user-friendly interface that can be accessed on different devices.
10. To develop the system using proper Software Engineering practices and the Scrum framework.
11. To continuously develop, test, review, and improve the system through multiple sprints.
12. To encourage the reuse of clothes by providing an organized platform for pre-owned clothing.

---

## 1.4 Target Users

The system will mainly support three types of users:

### Buyer
A user who searches for and views pre-owned clothes and contacts sellers about products they are interested in.

### Seller
A user who uploads and manages clothing listings and communicates with potential buyers.

### Administrator
A user responsible for managing the marketplace, monitoring listings, managing users, and handling reported or inappropriate content.

The main features of the proposed system include:

- User profile management

---

## 1.6 Project Scope

The project will focus on developing the core functionality required for an online pre-owned clothing marketplace.

The system will allow users to register, log in, create and manage clothing listings, browse products, search and filter clothes, view product information, and communicate with sellers.

The administrator will be able to manage users and listings and review reported content.

The initial version of the system will focus on the marketplace and communication between buyers and sellers. Physical delivery of clothes and direct financial transactions will not be included in the initial scope unless they are added later as an enhancement.

---

## 1.7 Expected Outcome

At the end of the project, the team expects to deliver a functional web-based marketplace specifically designed for girls to buy and sell pre-owned clothes.

The completed system should provide a clear and simple user experience for buyers and sellers while providing administrators with basic tools to manage the platform.

The project will also demonstrate the team's ability to apply Software Engineering concepts, including requirements engineering, system modeling, software development, testing, documentation, version control, and Scrum-based project management.

---

## 1.8 Development Methodology

The project will follow the Scrum framework. The development work will be divided into several sprints, with each sprint producing a usable increment of the system.

The Product Owner will maintain and prioritize the Product Backlog based on the importance of system requirements. The Scrum Master will facilitate Scrum activities and help the team follow the Scrum process. The Development Team will analyze requirements, design the system, develop the features, test the system, and maintain the technical documentation.

The team will conduct Daily Scrum meetings to discuss progress, planned work, and any problems. At the end of each sprint, the team will conduct a Sprint Review to evaluate the completed work and a Sprint Retrospective to identify improvements for the next sprint.

All major project documents, diagrams, meeting minutes, development progress, and other project artifacts will be maintained in the team's GitHub repository.

---

## 1.9 Project Benefits

The proposed system is expected to provide benefits to both users and the development team.

For buyers, the system will make it easier to find affordable pre-owned clothes and compare available products.

For sellers, the system will provide an organized way to advertise clothes they no longer need and reach potential buyers.

For the community, encouraging the reuse of clothing can help reduce unnecessary clothing waste and promote more sustainable consumption.

For the development team, the project provides practical experience in applying Software Engineering principles and Scrum methodology to the development of a real-world software system.
