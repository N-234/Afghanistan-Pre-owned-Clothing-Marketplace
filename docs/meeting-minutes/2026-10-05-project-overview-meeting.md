## 1. Purpose of the Meeting

The purpose of the meeting was to discuss the project idea, identify the main problem, define the target users, and agree on the overall direction of the project.

---

## 2. Project Idea

The team discussed developing a web-based marketplace specifically for girls to buy and sell pre-owned clothes.

The platform will provide an organized alternative to using social media pages and messaging groups for buying and selling clothes.

---

## 3. Problem Discussed

The team identified several problems with existing informal methods:

- Clothing posts can be difficult to find.
- Buyers may not have enough product information.
- It is difficult to search by size, price, or category.
- Sellers may have difficulty managing their listings.
- There is no central platform for managing second-hand clothing listings.

---

## 4. Target Users

The team agreed on three main types of users:

- **Buyer:** Searches for clothes and contacts sellers.
- **Seller:** Adds and manages clothing listings.
- **Administrator:** Manages users, listings, and reported content.

---

## 5. Main Features Discussed

The team discussed including:

- User registration and login
- User profiles
- Add and manage clothing listings
- Clothing images
- Search and filtering
- Product details
- Contact seller
- Mark items as sold
- Report inappropriate listings
- Administrator dashboard

---

## 6. Project Scope

The first version of the project will focus on the online marketplace.

Physical delivery and direct payment will not be included in the initial version.

---

## 7. Development Approach

The team agreed to use the Scrum framework.

The project will be divided into sprints, and the team will use:

- Sprint Planning
- Daily Scrum
- Sprint Review
- Sprint Retrospective

---

## 8. Decisions Made

The team agreed that the system should be:

- Simple and easy to use
- Focused on pre-owned clothes
- Designed specifically for girls
- Accessible on different devices
- Developed gradually using Scrum
