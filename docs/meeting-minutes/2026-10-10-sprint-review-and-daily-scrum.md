## Meeting Purpose

The purpose of this meeting was to review the progress of Sprint 1, demonstrate the current website, discuss the completed frontend and backend work, collect feedback from the Product Owner, and identify any remaining issues or improvements.

---

## 1. Sprint 1 Progress and Website Demonstration

### Nargis Bunyadi – Frontend Development

Nargis shared her screen and demonstrated the current version of the website.

She presented the main Sprint 1 frontend work, including:

- Homepage
- Website name and basic layout
- Clothing categories
- Login page
- Account creation
- Sell Clothes section
- Clothing information form
- Image upload
- Listing preview
- My Account option

She demonstrated how a user can log in, add clothing information, upload an image, and preview a listing.

Nargis also identified an issue with the current login flow. After a successful login, the system displays a success message but does not automatically redirect the user to the homepage or account page.

She explained that this part still needs improvement.

---

### Parwana Jafari – Backend Development

Parwana explained the backend work completed during Sprint 1.

She reported that she worked on:

- Backend routes for the main pages
- Connecting the backend with the HTML pages
- Making the website interactive
- User account creation
- Login functionality
- Database integration
- User session handling
- Storing website information in the database

She explained that the database currently includes two main tables:

- `users`
- `listings`

These tables are used to save user information and clothing listing information.

Parwana also confirmed that the login redirect still needs improvement and that some temporary preview messages will be removed later.

---

## 2. Product Owner Feedback

### Sara Qateh – Product Owner

Sara reviewed the website and gave positive feedback about the overall design and organization.

She especially liked the visual appearance and color combination of the website.

Sara confirmed that the main Sprint 1 requirements had been addressed.

She also suggested adding more clothing options and varieties to make the marketplace more attractive and useful for users.

The team discussed adding more clothing categories, including clothing suitable for weddings and special occasions.

The discussion highlighted that many girls may buy expensive clothes for special events and use them only once or twice, so the marketplace could provide a useful opportunity to resell these clothes.

---

## 3. Scrum Master Feedback

### Hajar Khawari – Scrum Master

Hajar said that the website looked good and that the team had made good progress.

She asked the developers whether they had faced any problems or blockers while working on Sprint 1.

The developers explained that there were no major coding blockers.

A small issue occurred while organizing the project files on GitHub, but it was resolved after clarification between the developers.

---

## General Discussion

- The team reviewed the completed Sprint 1 website work.
- Frontend and backend development were demonstrated.
- The login process still needs improvement.
- The My Account section will be improved.
- More clothing categories and options should be added.
- The team discussed adding wedding and special-event clothing.
- No major coding blockers were reported.
- A small GitHub organization issue was resolved.

---

## Key Decisions

- The current Sprint 1 website work was reviewed and accepted as good progress.
- The login flow should be improved so users are redirected after successful login.
- The My Account section should clearly show the logged-in user.
- More clothing options and categories should be added.
- Frontend and backend integration will continue to be improved.
- The team will apply the Product Owner’s feedback as improvements while preparing for Sprint 2.

---

## Blockers

No major blockers were reported.

A small GitHub file organization issue occurred during development, but it was resolved with help from the team.

---

## Next Steps

- Improve the login redirect after successful login.
- Improve the My Account section.
- Add more clothing categories and varieties.
- Remove temporary preview messages.
- Continue improving frontend and backend integration.
- Prepare for the Sprint 1 Retrospective and Sprint 2 Planning.
