# Sprint Review

## 🎯 Sprint 4 Goal

The main goal of Sprint 4 was **Administration and Safety**.

The Sprint focused on giving administrators the tools needed to manage reports, users, and listings while helping keep the marketplace safe.

A total of **23 Story Points** were planned for Sprint 4.

The Sprint included:

- US-015 – Report Listing
- US-016 – Review Reports
- US-017 – Manage Users
- US-018 – Monitor Listings
- NFR-004 – Administrator Access

---

## 💻 Sprint 4 Demonstration

### 👩‍💻 Nargis Bunyadi

Nargis shared her screen and demonstrated the Sprint 4 work.

The demonstration included:

- User account and seller dashboard
- Editing a listing
- Marking a listing as sold
- Reporting a listing from the marketplace
- Entering a reason for a report
- Submitting a report for administrator review
- Administrator dashboard
- Viewing registered users
- Deactivating user accounts
- Viewing submitted reports
- Removing inappropriate listings
- Viewing listing status such as:
  - Available
  - Sold
  - Removed

Nargis also explained that reported listings are stored with the related information so that administrators can review them.

The team showed that normal users cannot use the administrator functions and that administrator features are available through the admin area.

---

### 👩‍💻 Parwana Jafari

Parwana supported the demonstration and explained the administrator-side functionality.

She confirmed that the Sprint included:

- Administrator view
- Reviewing reports
- Managing users
- Removing listings when needed
- Viewing listing information and status
- Connecting the new Sprint 4 features with the existing system

The developers confirmed that the Sprint 4 work had been completed and that there was no remaining feature that needed to be added before moving to Sprint 5.

---

## 🧪 Testing

The team explained that features were tested during development.

Testing included:

- Trying features with different accounts
- Checking new functionality more than once
- Testing previous features again after adding new changes
- Checking login and registration
- Checking listing functionality
- Checking new administrator features together with existing features

The developers explained that they try to test both new and previous functionality after making changes so that existing features continue to work correctly.

---

## 👩‍💼 Product Owner Feedback

Sara reviewed the Sprint 4 result and said that the team had made good progress, especially on the reporting functionality.

She asked whether a confirmation message appears after a user submits a report.

The team explained that report submission feedback was already available in some cases, but they agreed to check it again and improve it if needed.

Sara also asked the team to continue testing every component carefully to make sure the website works correctly.

---

## 💡 Product Improvement for Sprint 5

One small improvement was identified during the Sprint Review:

- Make sure the user receives a clear confirmation message after submitting a report.

This improvement can be checked and improved during Sprint 5 together with the notification work.

## ✅ Sprint 4 Result

At the end of the Sprint Review:

- All **23 Story Points** were confirmed as completed.
- Sprint 4 was reviewed and accepted.
- The Sprint Goal was achieved.
- The team was ready to continue to Sprint 5.
