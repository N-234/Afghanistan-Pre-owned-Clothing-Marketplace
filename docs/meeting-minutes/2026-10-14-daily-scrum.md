### Parwana Jafari – Backend

Parwana reported progress on the backend for product listings and product information.

#### Completed Work

Parwana completed backend work for **Clothing Condition**.

The seller can choose the condition of a clothing item, such as:

- New
- Like New
- Good
- Used

The selected condition is saved with the product information and can be displayed to buyers.

This completes the backend functionality required for:

**US-007: Clothing Condition**

Parwana also completed backend work for **Product Photo Upload**.

Her work included:

- Handling uploaded clothing photos
- Saving uploaded photos with the related listing
- Connecting photos to product information
- Displaying product photos with listings
- Showing product photos on the homepage

This work supports:

**US-008: Upload Product Photos**

Parwana also continued working on product information and database integration.

She worked on retrieving product information from the database and displaying the correct listing information on the homepage.

This work supports:

**US-009: Product Information**

#### Development Issues

Parwana explained that she sometimes faces small coding issues during development, such as:

- Missing imports
- Syntax errors
- Incorrect brackets
- Incorrect commas
- Small mistakes that appear during testing

She explained that these are normal development issues and that she checks the code, finds the problem, and fixes it.

**Major Blockers:** None reported

## Proposed Improvement – Forgot Password

Nargis suggested adding a **Forgot Password** feature to the website.

She explained that users who forget their password should have an option to recover access to their account.

This feature would require:

- A frontend password recovery form
- Backend support for password recovery
- Coordination between frontend and backend

The feature was suggested during the meeting but has not yet been added to the current Sprint 2 backlog.
