# Part 1 – Sprint Review

## Meeting Purpose

The first part of the meeting was held to review the final Sprint 2 work, demonstrate the completed features, check the final result, receive feedback from the Product Owner, and confirm the completion of Sprint 2.

---

## Sprint Goal

The Sprint 2 goal was to improve product search and seller listings so buyers could find clothes more easily and sellers could add complete product information.

The planned Sprint 2 work included:

- US-005: Search for Clothes
- US-006: Size and Price Filters
- US-007: Clothing Condition
- US-008: Upload Product Photos
- US-009: Product Information
- NFR-002: Performance
- NFR-003: Input Validation

**Total Planned Story Points:** 33

---

## Opening – Hajar Khawari

Hajar welcomed the team and explained that October 16 was the final day of Sprint 2.

She explained that the meeting would have two parts:

1. Sprint Review – to review and demonstrate the final Sprint 2 work
2. Sprint Retrospective – to discuss the team's experience and improvements for the next Sprint

Hajar then asked Nargis and Parwana to share their screens and demonstrate the work completed during Sprint 2.

---

## Frontend Demonstration – Nargis Bunyadi

Nargis shared the frontend work she completed and tested during Sprint 2.

### Category Filtering

Nargis completed the Category Filter and demonstrated that it works together with the other filtering options.

The demonstration included:

- Filtering clothes by category
- Filtering by size
- Filtering by price
- Using Search together with the filters
- Using the Clear Filter option to reset the selected filters

The Category Filter JavaScript issue reported earlier in the Sprint was fixed.

### My Account Update

Nargis also updated the My Account section.

The account area now includes a small menu that allows the user to access the Logout option.

### Forgot Password and Reset Password UI

Nargis created the frontend forms for:

- Forgot Password
- Reset Password

She demonstrated frontend validation, including:

- Invalid email address messages
- Minimum password length validation
- Password confirmation validation
- Error message when the new passwords do not match

### Responsive Layout Testing

Nargis tested the website in a smaller browser window to check how the layout appears on smaller screens.

She also removed unnecessary preview messages from the Login and Signup pages.

### GitHub Update

The completed frontend changes were uploaded to GitHub and shared with the backend developer for integration.

---

## Backend Demonstration – Parwana Jafari

Parwana continued the demonstration by showing the working backend integration.

### Forgot Password Functionality

Parwana demonstrated the full Forgot Password process.

The process included:

1. Entering an incorrect password on the Login page
2. Opening the Forgot Password page
3. Entering the registered email address
4. Requesting a password reset link
5. Receiving the password reset link by email
6. Opening the Reset Password page
7. Creating a new password
8. Successfully updating the password
9. Logging in again using the new password

The complete password recovery process worked successfully during the demonstration.

The Forgot Password feature was an additional improvement introduced during Sprint 2 and was not part of the original Sprint 2 backlog.

---

## Search and Filtering Review

The team also reviewed the clothing search and filtering functionality.

The implemented options allow users to search and filter clothes using:

- Search
- Category
- Size
- Maximum Price

The filters were demonstrated working together.

Parwana also showed another layout option where clothing items were displayed in separate sections based on their categories.

The team discussed which category layout would be more suitable for the marketplace.

---

## Final Development Status

Before receiving Product Owner feedback, Hajar asked Nargis and Parwana whether any Sprint 2 work was still unfinished or required additional testing.

The developers confirmed that the planned Sprint 2 work was completed.

They confirmed that the Sprint covered:

- Search
- Filtering
- Clothing information
- Clothing condition

- Product validation

The team also completed the additional Forgot Password feature.

## Final Testing and Issues

The developers reported that no major errors remained at the end of testing.

## Product Owner Feedback – Sara Qateh

# Part 2 – Sprint Retrospective

## Retrospective Purpose

After completing the Sprint Review, the team continued with the Sprint Retrospective.

The purpose was to discuss:

- What went well during Sprint 2
- What difficulties the team faced
- What the team learned
- What could be improved in the next Sprint

---

## What Went Well

### Parwana Jafari

Parwana explained that Sprint 2 was easier to manage because the team had already gained experience from Sprint 1.

She said that:

- Sprint 1 helped her understand the development process better
- She became more familiar with how to complete the work
- Sprint 2 did not have any major problems
- She learned new things while working through the Sprint

---

### Nargis Bunyadi

Nargis explained that coding can sometimes be difficult and can include misunderstandings or technical problems.

However, she described Sprint 2 as a good learning experience.

She mentioned that:

- This was her first experience doing the frontend of a website
- She faced some development issues during the work
- She and Parwana worked together to solve problems
- The Sprint gave her useful practical frontend experience

---

### Sara Qateh

Sara said that she was proud of the team's progress and felt that the team was doing well with the project.

---

## Challenges

The team did not report any major unresolved problem during Sprint 2.

However, several general challenges were discussed:

- Coding can sometimes be complicated
- Some technical issues appeared during development
- The beginning of the overall project was more difficult because the team was still learning the process, roles, and project requirements
- Some features required frontend and backend coordination
- Additional setup was required for the Forgot Password functionality

The team explained that the project process had become easier as they gained more experience.

---

## Problems Resolved During the Sprint

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Category Filter JavaScript issue | Fixed and tested with other filters |
| Forgot Password library/import issue | Required library was installed and the issue was fixed |
| Frontend and backend integration | Developers coordinated and integrated their work |
| Filter testing | Search, category, size, and price filters were tested together |

---

## What the Team Learned

Sprint 2 helped the team become more familiar with the project development process.

The team learned that:

- Daily progress checking is useful
- Testing should start earlier
- Frontend and backend developers should communicate quickly when their work depends on each other
- Problems are easier to solve when developers work together
- Experience from previous Sprints makes future work easier

---

## Improvements for the Next Sprint

For the next Sprint, the team agreed to focus on:

- Checking progress every day
- Starting testing earlier
- Communicating quickly when frontend and backend tasks depend on each other
- Continuing teamwork when technical issues appear
- Identifying and fixing problems before the final Sprint day

---

## Key Decisions

- Sprint 2 was completed with all 33 planned story points.
- The Sprint 2 goal was achieved.
- The Sprint 2 result was reviewed and accepted.
- No major unresolved blockers remained at the end of the Sprint.
- Forgot Password and Reset Password were completed as additional improvements.
- The team will apply the lessons from Sprint 2 to improve the development process in Sprint 3.
- Testing and integration should begin earlier in future Sprints.

---

## Final Outcome

Sprint 2 was successfully completed.

The team completed all planned Sprint 2 requirements and demonstrated the final system during the Sprint Review.

The developers confirmed that the planned work was finished, the Product Owner provided positive feedback, and the Sprint Goal was achieved.

During the Retrospective, the team reflected on its progress, challenges, and learning experience and identified improvements to apply in Sprint 3.
