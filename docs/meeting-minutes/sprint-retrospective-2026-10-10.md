## Meeting Purpose

The purpose of this meeting was to reflect on Sprint 1, discuss what went well, identify any difficulties, and agree on what can be improved in Sprint 2.

---

## What Went Well

### Nargis Bunyadi – Frontend

Nargis explained that the frontend work in Sprint 1 went well.

She worked on the beginning parts of the website, including:

- Homepage
- Login page
- Basic clothing-related sections

She said that she did not face any major coding problems during the frontend work and that the first part of the development went smoothly.

---

### Parwana Jafari – Backend

Parwana explained that the backend work was more difficult at the beginning.

She mentioned that she had some problems with:

- Connecting the database to the backend
- Connecting the frontend to the backend

She said that as the work continued, she understood the structure better and became more comfortable working with the different pages.

She also explained that when small errors appeared, she tested the system, checked the issue, and fixed the errors.

---

### Sara Qateh – Product Owner

Sara said that the developers had done a good job in Sprint 1.

She said that the work was organized well and that she was happy with the progress.

She repeated her earlier suggestion that more clothing varieties should be added to make the marketplace better.

---

## What Could Be Improved

- Add more clothing varieties and options.
- Continue improving the connection between frontend and backend.
- Keep testing the system and fixing small errors when they appear.
- Continue improving the website step by step in Sprint 2.

---

## Key Learnings

- The frontend work went smoothly with no major coding issues.
- Backend integration was difficult at first, but the problems were solved through testing and practice.
- Communication between team members helped solve technical issues.
- More clothing variety can improve the user experience.

---

## Action Items for Sprint 2

- Nargis will continue improving the frontend and add more clothing varieties.
- Parwana will continue improving backend integration and fix any technical issues.
- The team will continue testing the website while developing new features.
- The Product Owner will continue giving feedback as the project develops.
