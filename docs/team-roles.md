### Meeting Schedule

- **Daily Scrum:** Every day at 6:00 PM for approximately 10 minutes
- **Sprint Planning:** Every Monday evening
- **Sprint Review:** Every Wednesday morning
- **Sprint Retrospective:** Every Saturday evening
  
---

### Definition of Done

A task or user story is *Done* when:

1. The assigned work is completed.
2. The work has been reviewed and tested.
3. Required documentation is updated.
4. Changes are committed and pushed to the GitHub repository.
5. The Product Owner confirms that the completed work meets the agreed requirements and acceptance criteria.
